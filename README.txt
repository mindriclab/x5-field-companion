 ENGLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IS THIS?
A free tool for Insta360 X5 owners. Two modules:

  1. GUIDE         - Practical knowledge on settings, modes
                     (PureVideo, Single-Lens), mounting, audio,
                     SD cards, troubleshooting and stock footage.
  2. SETUP PLANNER - Four questions (project, time of day,
                     environment, mount) produce a concrete setup
                     recommendation plus a matching packing list.
                     Fully rule-based, no AI, no internet required.

Plus: a cheatsheet (quick reference) and a post-shoot checklist.

USAGE
  - Just double-click "X5-Field-Companion.html" to open it in your browser.
  - Works fully offline. No installation, no login, no tracking.
  - Switch language (DE/EN) and theme (dark/light) at the bottom left.
  - Checklists and settings are stored locally in your browser.

RECOMMENDED BROWSERS
  Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
